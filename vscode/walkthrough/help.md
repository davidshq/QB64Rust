# Look a word up

Every statement, function and keyword has a page in the help, which comes with the extension and works offline.

- **Search Help** finds a page by its name or by what it does: try `random` or `circle`.
- In your program, right-click a word such as `PRINT` and choose **QB64: Help for This Word**.
- Hold the mouse over a word to see what it does in one sentence, with a link to its page.

A page opens in a tab beside your code. Its examples have **Run this example**, so you can see one work.
